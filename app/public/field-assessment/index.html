The evidence

What was measured, in the order it happened: the dry season, the fires, and the first rain.

Dry season

The HH radar echo fell through the dry season on open peat, measured against closed forest. The radar sees the ground through cloud and smoke.

A sketch, not data

Fire

Fire detections come from NASA FIRMS and are a separate measurement. The radar’s coherence also drops where and when FIRMS saw fire; other changes to the ground lower it too.

A sketch, not data

First rain

On the first pass after the rain the echo rose again on most blocks. On some it stayed low. That says where to look, not why.

A sketch, not data

Still low after the rain

Where the echo stayed low, compare it on site with the fire footprint, the vegetation and the water level.

At each block, record

Visit one unlisted block nearby on the same day, and record the same four things, so there is something to compare with.

What this list cannot tell you
  • Nothing here was checked on the ground. It is one season of satellite data.
  • It does not say why a block changed, measure the water table, or recommend any work on a canal.
  • The pass after the rain is a single pass per orbit, and fire detections can miss small or hidden fires.
