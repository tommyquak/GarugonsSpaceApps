# The demo slides

Seven slides, the demo limit. The thread through all seven: the dry season in three beats, **fall, fire, rain**, seen by a radar that does not stop for cloud or smoke.

## The deck as built

The deck is made by `submission/build_slides.py` (a `.pptx` for Google Slides) and `submission/build_html_deck.py` (one HTML page). As built on 14 November 2026:

1. Title, the takeaway and the problem, with the one-look map.
2. Cloud and smoke against the radar on one day, and how the radar sees a dry season (the animation).
3. The season in one picture, three numbers, and the six choices behind the method with the reason for each.
4. The app in four recordings: the season, "Where to check" in 3D, the ontology under "How it decides", the field assessment.
5. Why believe it: two orbits agree, and the audit.
6. The limits, each with our answer, and the water-table bridge.
7. What comes next (ground truth; subsidence as a second layer, as a plan; more seasons and other peatlands), and the links.

Build the final deck with `--final`; all eight numbers it reads are `final` entries. The pictures and recordings come from the release `figures-v3`.
