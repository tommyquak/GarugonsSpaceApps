# References and data provenance

Tags describe the scope of each cited source: **[E]** established for the conditions studied; **[S]** a standard method or interpretation whose assumptions still need checking here; **[H]** a project hypothesis to test. A published result does not validate this team's maps or numbers.

## Science and methods

- **[E]** Putra, S. S., Holden, J., & Baird, A. J. (2021). “The effects of ditch dams on water-level dynamics in tropical peatlands.” *Hydrological Processes*, 35, e14174. DOI: [10.1002/hyp.14174](https://doi.org/10.1002/hyp.14174). Field measurements at forested, drained, and dammed Sebangau sites show that water levels vary with site and season; this is regional hydrological context, not evidence that our radar measures water table or isolates canal effects.
- **[E]** Uhde, A., et al. (2026). “Monitoring water level dynamics in neotropical peatlands with earth observation data.” *Remote Sensing of Environment*, 337, 115342. DOI: [10.1016/j.rse.2026.115342](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2026.115342). Evaluates L-band HH backscatter against water levels across neotropical peatland sites and reports site- and vegetation-dependent relationships; it does not validate NISAR in Kalimantan.
- **[E]** Murdiyarso, D., Román-Cuesta, R. M., Verchot, L. V., Herold, M., Gumbricht, T., Herold, N., & Martius, C. (2017). “New map reveals more peat in the tropics.” DOI: [10.17528/cifor/006452](https://doi.org/10.17528/cifor/006452). Describes a modelled tropical wetlands/peat map and identifies field validation as necessary for newly mapped peat areas.
- **[S]** Spearman, C. (1904). “The Proof and Measurement of Association between Two Things.” *The American Journal of Psychology*, 15(1), 72–101. DOI: [10.2307/1412159](https://doi.org/10.2307/1412159). Foundation for rank association; a rank correlation measures association of ranks, not agreement or causation.
- **[S]** Hemerik, J., & Goeman, J. (2018). “Exact testing with random permutations.” *TEST*, 27, 811–825. DOI: [10.1007/s11749-017-0571-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11749-017-0571-1). Provides a basis for permutation tests under an appropriate invariance/exchangeability null; a date-shuffle test must state its null and preserve any relevant time-series structure.
- **[H]** The project's testable hypothesis is that seasonal NISAR backscatter change can help screen for land drying in mapped peatlands. It is an observed radar-change product, not a direct measurement of peat moisture, water table, subsidence, fire ignition, or canal causality.

## Data provenance

- **[E] NISAR L2 GCOV:** NASA/ISRO geocoded polarimetric covariance product; project pipeline builds 50 m HH/HV stacks from the GCOV acquisitions described in [`pipeline/radar/README.md`](../pipeline/radar/README.md) and [`docs/DATA_CONTRACT.md`](DATA_CONTRACT.md). Official sources: [NASA NISAR sample data/specifications](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/nisar/sample-data/) and [ASF GCOV product guide](https://github.com/ASFOpenSARlab/nisar-docs/blob/main/data-products/level-2/gcov.md). GCOV supplies calibrated gamma-nought power; the team's stacks are derived, and track dates differ.
- **[E] GPM IMERG Late daily V07:** product `GPM_3IMERGDL_07`, DOI [10.5067/GPM/IMERGDL/DAY/07](https://doi.org/10.5067/GPM/IMERGDL/DAY/07); [NASA product record](https://disc.gsfc.nasa.gov/datasets/GPM_3IMERGDL_07/summary) and [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md). Daily gridded precipitation is rainfall context, not a local gauge or water-table observation; spatial averaging and product uncertainty limit use for defining a local rain transition.
- **[E] NASA FIRMS active fire:** the project plan specifies MODIS and VIIRS detections; see [FIRMS](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/) and the [NASA VIIRS 375 m NRT product record](https://modaps.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/services/about/products/viirs-land-c2-nrt/vnp14imgtdl_nrt.html), DOI [10.5067/FIRMS/VIIRS/VNP14IMGT_NRT.002](https://doi.org/10.5067/FIRMS/VIIRS/VNP14IMGT_NRT.002). A detection is a sensor observation of a thermal anomaly; finite resolution and missed detections mean absence is not evidence of no fire. The project's distinct-day-within-200 m aggregation is its own method.
- **[S] Gumbricht et al. (2017), “An expert system model for mapping tropical wetlands and peatlands reveals South America as the largest contributor.”** The model provides an independently derived boundary for testing how peat-area estimates change with the map. Track 3 reports the comparison as exploratory. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/gcb.13689) · [Track 3 comparison](https://github.com/tommyquak/GarugonsSpaceApps/pull/44)
- **[E] CIFOR tropical and subtropical wetlands distribution:** Gumbricht et al. (2017), dataset DOI [10.17528/CIFOR/DATA.00058](https://doi.org/10.17528/CIFOR/DATA.00058), [CIFOR-ICRAF data record](https://www.foreststreesagroforestry.org/data-portal/swamp/tropical-and-subtropical-wetlands-distribution/). Modelled wetlands/peat extent is a comparison mask, not field truth for each analysis cell; cite the exact file/version used.
- **[E] Indonesia Ministry of Agriculture peat polygons:** the project plan identifies the Global Forest Watch map service as the access point; see [Global Forest Watch data](https://data.globalforestwatch.org/). Use as a separate mapped boundary, not ground truth. Confirm the exact layer and its redistribution licence before sharing the data or derived masks.
- **[E] OpenStreetMap waterways:** project-plan source for canal context; [OpenStreetMap copyright and licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Attribute contributors under ODbL. Mapped waterways may be incomplete and should not be treated as a verified canal inventory.

[Track 3’s data-v6 release](https://github.com/tommyquak/GarugonsSpaceApps/releases/tag/data-v6) contains both daily IMERG series and the source keys `last_dry_day_west` and `last_dry_day_east`. Its [onset rule](https://github.com/tommyquak/GarugonsSpaceApps/blob/data-v6/pipeline/context/rain.py) identifies the first day with at least 5 mm that starts a five-day period with at least 25 mm after the driest 30 days. This timestamps the comparison; the original [data-v5 recovery layers](https://github.com/tommyquak/GarugonsSpaceApps/releases/tag/data-v5) measure the radar response.

Report any project statistic only from its corresponding entry in `pipeline/out/validation.json`, with its recorded status. Keep all areas and shares separated by peat mask and off-mask as required by [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md); describe rainfall, fire detections, and peat boundaries with the limitations above. See [`docs/LIMITS.md`](LIMITS.md).

- **[E] PEATMAP:** Xu, J., Morris, P. J., Liu, J., & Holden, J. (2018), [PEATMAP dataset](https://doi.org/10.5518/252), CC BY 4.0. The team's [data-v7 rasters](https://github.com/tommyquak/GarugonsSpaceApps/releases/tag/data-v7) delineate mapped peat for these comparisons; agreement with the official map does not establish an independent field boundary.
- **[S] Released inputs for the app:** [data-v9](https://github.com/tommyquak/GarugonsSpaceApps/releases/tag/data-v9) supplies the rain-free start, drying and recovery layers, dated rainfall, fire-footprint layers and validation seed. [Data-v3](https://github.com/tommyquak/GarugonsSpaceApps/releases/tag/data-v3) identifies the earlier map and observation window; its numbers do not describe v9.
